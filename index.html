Why we started Beacon

Some of the best things a student does happen where few people are watching.

What we hope to notice

A student might check on a friend, take care of things at home, or keep doing the right thing when it costs them something. We think the people who do those things deserve to hear what their choices have meant to others.

In a nomination or an application, tell us what you’ve seen or lived through. An honest account of an ordinary day can tell us more about someone’s character than a list of accomplishments can.

Why a nomination matters

Putting a student’s name forward is a way of saying, “I’ve noticed how you treat people and what you’re working toward, and I think it matters.” If you received a nomination, we hope you take that seriously. Someone went out of their way to say it.

Students who apply directly have the same opportunity to be considered. You can tell us about your life in your own words.

How selection works

A nomination and a direct application are both official entries and receive the same consideration.

  1. Enter Beacon Scholars

    Eligible students are in grades 9–12 and have an unweighted GPA of at least 2.5. Apply yourself or have someone submit a nomination on your behalf. If you were nominated, use your private Entry Key to continue that entry in your own Beacon account.

  2. Complete your entry

    Beacon checks eligibility and verifies one academic and one character reference. Students under 18 also need parent or guardian consent. If you have an entry linked to your account, use your dashboard to check your next steps.

  3. Reach finalist consideration

    Finalists are the students who reach the last round of consideration. Beacon looks at the story behind each entry, including how students treat others, handle responsibilities, and respond when things get difficult. Reaching this stage is an achievement worth sharing with the people who have encouraged you.

  4. Select the inaugural class

    Beacon will name two students as Scholars for the Class of 2027. Each will receive an official letter and certificate from The Prayer Project.

Common questions

What do I do if I received a nomination email or letter?

Sign in with a verified Beacon account and enter your private Entry Key. Open your nomination in My Dashboard and add any outstanding references. If you’re under 18, enter your parent or guardian’s information so they can complete a separate consent form.

Continue your nomination →
Can I nominate someone privately?

Yes. A named nomination allows Beacon to share your name with the student. With a confidential nomination, Beacon administrators keep your name private. You can also submit anonymously without signing in.

Who should I ask to be a reference?

Ask one person who knows your schoolwork and one who can speak to your character. A qualified nominator can sometimes serve as one reference after Beacon verifies their identity and accepts their statement. Anonymous nominations still need two separate references.

Do I need volunteer hours to apply?

No. Community service is optional. You can tell us about the responsibilities you carry, the way you care for people, or something difficult that helped you grow.

How many students will be named Beacon Scholars?

PROGRAM PARTICIPATION

Privacy, care & integrity.

This is an honorary recognition program of The Prayer Project. No financial assistance, award amount, future scholarship, or academic accreditation is promised.

Nominations are valid official entries; students do not need to reapply. Final selection requires eligibility verification, two verified references and parent/guardian consent for minors. Committee reviews are confidential; donors do not select recipients.

Nomination privacy choices include named, confidential, and fully anonymous. Public recognition and photos are optional and separately authorized. Private addresses, references and guardian records are never included in public profiles.

Eligibility and consent

Beacon considers students in grades 9–12 with an unweighted GPA of at least 2.5. Service is optional. If a student is under 18, Beacon verifies a parent or guardian’s authorization before final recognition.

How Beacon uses your information

Beacon uses entries to verify eligibility, follow up with students and authorized adults, review entries, and deliver recognition. Share only information relevant to the entry. Avoid including private health information, home addresses, or school records in an essay or nomination.

Corrections and withdrawal

Students can check their entry in the Beacon dashboard. Contact The Prayer Project to request a correction, ask about your records, or withdraw guardian consent. Include your entry ID when available and keep private documents out of your initial message.
