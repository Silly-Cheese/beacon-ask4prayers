04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Does a nomination count as an application?

Yes. A nomination is an official entry by itself. The student does not have to submit another application.

Can I nominate someone anonymously?

Yes. Nominators may choose named, confidential or fully anonymous submission. A fully anonymous submission does not count as a verified reference, so two separate references will be required before final selection.

Are two references required?

Two verified references are required before final selection. They may be obtained after the initial entry is received. A verified qualified nominator may count as one reference.

Are parents or guardians involved?

Beacon plans to obtain guardian authorization for participants under 18 before final recognition. A separate permission will be required for public photos or stories.

Will students receive money?

Beacon currently focuses on recognition. No monetary awards are available or promised at this stage.

PROGRAM PARTICIPATION

Privacy, care & integrity.

This is an honorary recognition program of The Prayer Project. No financial assistance, award amount, future scholarship, or academic accreditation is promised.

Nominations are valid official entries; students do not need to reapply. Final selection requires eligibility verification, two verified references and parent/guardian consent for minors. Committee reviews are confidential; donors do not select recipients.

Nomination privacy choices include named, confidential, and fully anonymous. Public recognition and photos are optional and separately authorized. Private addresses, references and guardian records are never included in public profiles.

Before public intake opens, publish complete policies covering eligibility (including the program's 2.5 GPA standard), notices, minor privacy, consent withdrawal, retention, and complaint review.
